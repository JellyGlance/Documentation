# Icon sources

- Original JellyGlance branding and selfh.st integration icons were copied from the JellyGlance documentation.
- Icons in `docs/icons/brands/` are Simple Icons distributed with the installed MkDocs Material theme. They retain their embedded titles and SVG geometry; brand colours were applied for visibility. Source: https://simpleicons.org/ (CC0; individual brand trademarks remain with their owners).
- Existing remote integration logos use the dashboard-icons URLs retained from the source documentation.
- Additional integration PNGs in `docs/icons/brands/` were downloaded from `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/selfhst/icons/png/`. Upstream: https://github.com/selfhst/icons. Brand marks belong to their respective owners.
